About & disclaimers

Reference-build controls

Not part of the shipped product. Present so this build can be tested against the Base44 version.

Live parsing runs through the /api/invoke-llm serverless function, which holds the API key server-side. No key is ever stored in the browser. Opened as a local file with no server, the app stays in demo mode.

Appearance

Automatic follows your device. Choose Light or Dark to override it — useful if your phone is dark all day but you read numbers more comfortably on white.

Text size

Scales the whole interface, so spacing and tap targets grow with the text instead of the layout breaking. Your browser's own zoom still works too.

Your data

Everything you log stays on this device, in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account. Clearing your browser data removes it too.

Take it with you

A one-page summary is the thing to print or email before an appointment. The spreadsheet is every item you've logged, in case you want the raw numbers.

Remove it

Delete everything?

This removes your meals, lab entries, targets, and settings from this device. It can't be undone.

Check a food label

Type or paste the ingredient list from a package. RenalRoute looks for the additives that matter for kidneys — the ones that never show up on the nutrition panel.
