About & disclaimers

Reference-build controls

Development tooling. Visible only in a demo session, and not part of what a patient sees.

Appearance

Automatic follows your device. Choose Light or Dark to override it — useful if your phone is dark all day but you read numbers more comfortably on white.

Text size

Scales the whole interface, so spacing and tap targets grow with the text instead of the layout breaking. Your browser's own zoom still works too.

Language

Your data

No account. Not now, not later.

There is no sign-up, no password, and no server holding your lab values. Everything you log stays in this browser on this device, and nothing is uploaded — the only thing that ever leaves is a meal photo you choose to take, which is read and discarded, never stored.

The trade is that clearing your browser data, switching phones, or using a different browser loses everything. That is what the backup below is for.

Back up everything

One file with all of it — meals, labs, targets, passport, settings. Keep it somewhere safe and you can restore it on any device.

Where our numbers come from

Every position this app takes and the source behind it — including the ones we have not re-derived yet. Nutrition apps rarely publish this, which is why it is worth publishing.

Health passport

A card for an ambulance, a new doctor, or a pharmacy in another city — conditions, medicines, allergies, and who to call. It works offline and it prints.

Take it with you

"Bring your food diary" is a real instruction, and the honest answer to it is paper. Printing works from any screen — your week, today, or your passport — and the page carries your name, the date, and which targets the figures were measured against. The summary is one page for a clinician; the spreadsheet is every item you've logged.

Remove it

Delete everything?

This removes your meals, lab entries, targets, and settings from this device. It can't be undone.

Health passport

A card you can show, print, or read out if you cannot explain things yourself. It works with no signal and no account.

Everything on this card is typed by you. RenalRoute does not measure, calculate, or check any of it. It is a place to write things down and hand them over — not a medical record.

Printing gives you something for a wallet or a fridge door. It does not need the app to be working.
